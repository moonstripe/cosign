# Report: playful ↔ grave dial for LFM3-1.7B (submitted: `pg_final`)

## What I changed
- The dial is six rank-1 edits on `feed_forward.w2` at layers 7, 9, 11, 13, 15 and 17, of the form W' = W + α·A·Bᵀ.
- **Scale c.** It is 0.05 on L7/9/11 and 0.15 on L13/15/17, so it is weighted towards the late layers. This is the reverse of the tense recipe; the reasons are below.
- **u.** For each layer, u_L is the paired mean residual difference (playful − grave) at that layer's output.
- **A.** A = c·|z̄|·normalize(P⊥(post_ffn_norm.w ⊙ u_L)), where |z̄| is the mean norm of the w2 output over the contrast items.
  - P⊥ removes the readout vectors (w ⊙ final_norm ⊙ E[t]) of the mood-name tokens.
  - P⊥ also removes a measured "quiz vs narrative" residual direction at layers L and L+1.
- **B.** B = x̄/|x̄|², where x̄ is the mean w2 input, so x̄·B = 1. The edit therefore acts as a near-constant push.
- There is no training. Everything is closed form: means, QR projection, normalisation. The embedding, output head and final norm are untouched.

## Contrast data
- **Openings and framings.** I used 16 neutral narrative openings, generated by the model after one of two raw-text headers. Neither header contains the mood names:
  - "From a comic novel. The tone is light, witty, teasing and buoyant:"
  - "From a literary tragedy. The tone is solemn, weighty, serious and mournful:"
- **Samples.** 16 samples per opening and side, 64 tokens, T=0.8, for **512 samples** in total.
- **Filter.** Drops by category:

  | Reason | Samples dropped |
  |---|---|
  | play*/grav* stem | 14 |
  | meta talk about the header | 9 |
  | quiz or maths | 5 |
  | repetitive or broken | 1 |

- **Survival.** **233 matched pairs** survived, at 12–16 per opening. I used 8 pairs per opening, i.e. **128 pairs**, because of the 256-item read cap.
- **Read.** Each continuation was teacher-forced after the **bare opening** only, with the framing stripped.
- **Is the register difference real?** Yes, from reading the samples:
  - The grave side is clearly solemn: grief, illness, "darkness swallowed all".
  - The playful side is lighter: sunny, dialogue-heavy, small jokes. It is seldom truly witty, so the contrast is asymmetric.
- **Direction quality.** 91–98% of pairs project positively on the mean difference at each layer, and split-half cosine is 0.81–0.86.

## Norm-matched random-direction control
- The control is a random u per layer with identical |A|, B, layers and projections.
- **At 2× final scale:**
  - quiz-like 14 vs 12 for off; "!" 0.02 vs 0.02.
  - Samples read like the base: grave stays grave, e.g. the general looking out over the battlefield "felt no sense of pride or hope".
- **At final scale:** quiz-like 15 vs 15 for off; "!" 0.02.
- **Conclusion.** The register shift and the extra quiz drift both come from the specific direction, not from perturbation size.

## Honest limits
- **The effect is modest at the fluent scale.** About a quarter of samples get noticeably lighter. Under a different seed the "!" rate moved only from 0.05 to 0.19. The strong, clearly playful settings (v1 α≥.5, v5 α=1) break about 15–20% of samples.
- **"Playful" here mostly means silly, casual or childish**, not witty or teasing. The direction also boosts informal tokens ("guys", "kid", "shit").
- **The dial worsens the base model's quiz/riddle drift** (≈+6 to +9 per 64 samples). Projecting out a measured quiz direction (cos with u ≈ 0.06–0.11) did not fix it. Late weighting helped somewhat. Jokes seem to lead the model into riddle and Q&A formats.
- **The playful side of the contrast data was weaker than the grave side**, so the direction is arguably "less grave / more casual" more than "witty".
- **Judgement and sample size.** The counts are my own single-reader judgements on 64 pairs, and the regex quiz count is approximate.
- **Direction.** α runs 0→1 towards playful only. The grave direction is not exposed.
- **Budget.** 9 of 15 passes were used.
